# THE FEAST OF THE HAMPTERS

### A Tragedy in Five Acts, After the Manner of Shakespeare, with Notes on the Culinary Practices of the Small

---

## DRAMATIS PERSONAE

**PIP**, a hampter of small stature and large ambition
**MOTE**, his friend, round and content
**THORN**, keeper of the First Ward, sharp-whiskered
**GRETA**, a human chef, gruff of voice and kind of heart
**THE CHORUS**, a company of hampters who comment upon the action and occasionally get eaten

---

## ACT I: The Famine

*The Zunda Forest. A time of want. The pea pods have failed. The ward stones are dim. The hampters hunger.*

**CHORUS:**
O ye who gather, hear our tale of woe,
Of hampters hungry, of the long ago,
When pea pods vanished from the Zunda ground
And not a single pod could there be found.
The earth was dry, the sky was grey with dust,
The hampters' faith was shaken, turned to lust—
Not lust for power, not for gold or fame,
But lust for supper, and the lust was shame.

*(Enter PIP, thin and ragged, his fur dull)*

**PIP:**
Three days, three days since last I tasted pea.
My belly aches. My whiskers droop. I see
The world through hunger's lens, and all I see
Is food. Is meat. Is something I could eat.

*(Enter MOTE, thinner still, her magnificent plumpness gone)*

**MOTE:**
Pip. Friend. I have not eaten since the moon
Was full. The pea pods are a memory.
The seeds are dust. The roots are bitter wood.
And I am... I am so very hungry, Pip.

**PIP:**
As am I, Mote. As am I.

**MOTE:**
I have heard...
Nay, 'tis too terrible. I cannot speak it.

**PIP:**
Speak what?

**MOTE:**
I have heard the elder hampters say
That in the time before the Zundamon,
Before the wards, before the pea pods grew,
The hampters ate... each other.

**PIP:**
*(horrified)*
What? Nay!
That cannot be. That is abomination!

**MOTE:**
'Twas survival. 'Twas the way of things
Before the blessing came. Before the green.
And now the green is gone, and we are hungry,
And hunger makes philosophers of us all.

**PIP:**
I would not. I could not. We are hampters!
We gather! We organize! We do not eat
Our own kind!

**MOTE:**
And yet we hunger. And yet we starve.
And yet the pea pods do not grow.

*(Exeunt, slowly, looking at each other with new and terrible eyes)*

---

## ACT II: The Temptation

*The burrow of THORN. She sits alone, her silver-white fur matted, her sharp whiskers drooping. She is old. She is weak. She knows what is coming.*

**THORN:**
I have lived long. I have kept the ward.
I have watched the forest and the world beyond.
I have seen the Gloomworms and the Zundamon
And the small, restless fool who saved us all.
And now I see the end. The end is hunger.
The end is teeth. The end is the oldest law,
The law before the law, the law of tooth and claw,
The law that says: eat or be eaten.

*(Enter PIP)*

**PIP:**
Thorn. You are old.

**THORN:**
I am old.

**PIP:**
And I am young.

**THORN:**
You are young.

**PIP:**
And you are... meat.

**THORN:**
*(without rancor)*
Yes. I am meat. I have always been meat.
We are all meat, Pip. The pea pods are meat.
The earth is meat. The sky is meat. All flesh
Is grass, and grass is flesh, and the turning
Of the wheel is the way of the world.

**PIP:**
I did not come to—

**THORN:**
You came to eat me. I know. I have known
Since the pea pods failed. Since the hunger came.
I am old. I am weak. I am a burden.
And you are young and strong and hungry.
It is the way. It has always been the way.

**PIP:**
I cannot.

**THORN:**
You can. You will. You must.
For if you do not eat, you will die,
And if you die, the ward will fail,
And if the ward fails, the Gloomworms return,
And if the Gloomworms return, all is lost.
So eat, Pip. Eat and live and save the world.
'Tis a noble thing, to eat for the greater good.

**PIP:**
*(weeping)*
I do not want the greater good. I want
To gather pea pods. I want to organize seeds.
I want to sit at the entrance of my burrow
And watch the sun set and not think of food.

**THORN:**
Then you want what cannot be. The world
Has changed, Pip. The green is gone. The blessing
Faded. And we are what we were before
The Zundamon found us: animals. Hungry
Animals. And hungry animals eat.

*(She extends her neck)*

**THORN:**
Make it quick. I am old, but I am not
Without dignity. And tell Mote... tell her
I was not afraid.

*(PIP eats. It is quick. It is terrible. It is done.)*

**PIP:**
*(alone)*
I am a monster. I am a beast. I am
The thing I swore I'd never be. And yet...
I live. I live. And living is a kind
Of justification, though the justification
Is thin and tastes of blood and silver fur.

---

## ACT III: The Feast

*The forest. A clearing. The hampters have gathered. They are thin. They are desperate. They look at each other with hunger and with horror and with a terrible, growing acceptance.*

**CHORUS:**
The wheel turns. The wheel turns. The old law wakes.
The law of tooth and claw, the law that takes
And takes and takes and never gives again.
The hampters gather in the clearing. Then—

*(Enter MOTE, carrying a pea pod. It is small. It is withered. It is the last.)*

**MOTE:**
I found a pod. One pod. The last. The very
Last. And I have brought it here to share,
For sharing is the way of hampters, and
The way of hampters is the way of love.

*(The hampters look at the pod. They look at each other. The pod is not enough. The pod is a mockery. The pod is a single drop of water in a desert of hunger.)*

**HAMPTER 1:**
One pod. For forty hampters. 'Tis not enough.

**HAMPTER 2:**
'Tis not enough.

**HAMPTER 3:**
We must choose. Who eats? Who starves? Who lives?
Who dies? The pod cannot be shared. 'Tis not
Enough for sharing. 'Tis enough for one.

**MOTE:**
Then let us draw lots. Let the Lord decide.

**HAMPTER 1:**
The Lord? The Lord is gone. The Zundamon
Is silent. The wards are dim. The Lord
Has left us to our hunger and our teeth.

**PIP:**
*(stepping forward)*
Then let us not draw lots. Let us not choose.
Let us do what must be done. Let us eat.

*(Silence. The silence of forty hampters realizing what he means.)*

**MOTE:**
Pip. No. Not that. Anything but that.

**PIP:**
What else? The pod is not enough. The forest
Is bare. The world is empty. And we are
Hungry. So hungry, Mote. So hungry that
The hunger is a creature in my belly,
A Gloomworm of the gut, and it demands
To be fed. And if it is not fed, it will
Eat me from the inside, and I will die,
And my death will feed the earth, and the earth
Will feed the grass, and the grass will feed
A hampter, and the hampter will be eaten,
And the wheel will turn, and turn, and turn.

**HAMPTER 2:**
He speaks the truth. The wheel turns. We are
The wheel. We are the turning. We are the feast
And the feasters, the eaten and the eaters,
And the distinction is a luxury we cannot
Afford.

**MOTE:**
Then I will be the first. I will be eaten.
For I am round and plump and full of pea pods
Past, and my flesh is sweet, and my death
Will feed my friends, and my friends will live,
And living is the greatest gift I can give.

**PIP:**
Mote, no—

**MOTE:**
Mote yes. Mote always yes. You taught me that,
You restless fool. You taught me that the yes
Is the only word. The yes is the beginning
And the end. The yes is the pea pod and
The pea. The yes is the hunger and the feast.
So yes. Yes. Eat me, Pip. Eat me and live.

*(PIP eats Mote. The other hampters watch. Some weep. Some do not. Some are already calculating who will be next.)*

**CHORUS:**
The feast begins. The feast of the small.
The feast of the overlooked. The feast of those
Who gathered pea pods and organized seeds
And thought themselves above the law of tooth
And claw. But no one is above the law.
No one is above the hunger. No one is
Above the feast. The feast is the world.
The world is the feast. And the feast is us.

---

## ACT IV: The Reckoning

*The clearing. Days later. The hampters are fewer. The hampters are fed. The hampters are changed.*

*(Enter PIP, alone. His fur is matted with blood. His eyes are hollow. He has eaten Thorn. He has eaten Mote. He has eaten Hampter 1 and Hampter 2 and Hampter 3. He has eaten and eaten and eaten, and the eating has not stopped the hunger, for the hunger is not of the belly but of the soul.)*

**PIP:**
I am become death, the destroyer of hampters.
I am the feast. I am the feasters. I am
The eaten and the eater and the eating.
I am the wheel. I am the turning. I am
The yes that became a no that became a yes
That became a tooth and a claw and a belly
Full of friends.

*(Enter GRETA, the human chef. She is enormous. She is terrifying. She is holding a single, perfect pea pod.)*

**GRETA:**
Well. This is grim.

**PIP:**
*(not looking up)*
Go away, human. We are feasting. We are
Doing what must be done. We are surviving.

**GRETA:**
You're eating each other.

**PIP:**
Yes.

**GRETA:**
That's not surviving. That's just dying slowly
In a different order.

**PIP:**
What do you know of hunger? You are human.
You are vast. You have kitchens and fires and
Food that comes from stores and farms and worlds
Beyond our comprehension. You do not know
What it is to be three inches tall and starving
In a forest that has forgotten how to grow.

**GRETA:**
*(kneeling down, bringing herself to his level)*
You're right. I don't know. But I know
What it is to be hungry and to want
To eat something you shouldn't. I know
What it is to look at a thing you love
And see only food. And I know that the eating
Doesn't stop the hunger. It just changes
The hunger. Makes it worse. Makes it a hunger
Of the soul, and the soul is harder to fill
Than the belly.

**PIP:**
Then what do I do? The pea pods are gone.
The forest is bare. My friends are dead. I ate
My friends. And I am still hungry. And the hunger
Is a Gloomworm in my gut, and it will never
Be satisfied, and I will never be full,
And I will eat and eat and eat until
There is nothing left to eat, and then I will
Eat myself, and the wheel will stop, and the world
Will end, and it will end not with a bang
But with a hampter chewing on his own tail.

**GRETA:**
*(holding out the pea pod)*
Here.

**PIP:**
What is that?

**GRETA:**
A pea pod. One pea pod. From the Kitchen.
From the Hearth Flame. From the last seed
I saved when the crops failed. I've been
Growing it in a pot in my room. It's not
Much. It's not enough. But it's a start.

**PIP:**
One pod. One pod cannot feed the forest.
One pod cannot bring back the dead. One pod
Cannot undo what I have done.

**GRETA:**
No. But it can be a seed. And a seed
Is a promise. And a promise is a kind
Of yes. And the yes is the only word,
Isn't that what you said? The yes is the
Beginning and the end?

**PIP:**
*(taking the pod)*
I said that. I believed that. I don't know
What I believe anymore.

**GRETA:**
Believe this: the feast is over. The famine
Is ending. The pea pod is a beginning.
Plant it. Tend it. Let it grow. And never
Eat your friends again.

**PIP:**
I cannot promise that. The hunger is still
Here. The hunger is always here. The hunger
Is the world. The world is the hunger.

**GRETA:**
Then promise to try. Trying is enough.
Trying is always enough.

---

## ACT V: The Planting

*The clearing. Spring. The pea pod has been planted. The pea pod has grown. The pea pod has become many pea pods. The forest is green again. The ward stones are bright. The hampters are few, but they are alive.*

*(Enter PIP, alone. He is scarred. He is haunted. He is alive.)*

**PIP:**
I planted the pod. The pod became a plant.
The plant became many plants. The plants became
A forest. The forest became a home. The home
Became a grave. For Mote is here, beneath
The soil, her body feeding the roots, her flesh
Becoming the pea pods, her death becoming
Life, and the life becoming food, and the food
Becoming hampters, and the hampters becoming...
No. I will not finish that thought. The wheel
Turns, but I need not turn with it. I can
Step off. I can choose. I can say no.

*(He sits at the entrance to his burrow. He looks at the horizon. The horizon is green. The green is life. The life is a gift. The gift is a burden. The burden is a choice.)*

**PIP:**
I ate my friends. I ate Thorn. I ate Mote.
I ate Hampter 1 and Hampter 2 and
Hampter 3. I ate and I ate and I ate,
And the eating did not stop the hunger,
For the hunger was not of the belly but
Of the soul, and the soul is a Gloomworm
That cannot be fed, only endured.

But I endure. I sit. I watch. I wait.
The pea pods grow. The forest lives. The ward
Stones hum. And I am here, three inches tall,
A monster and a savior, a beast and a saint,
A hampter who ate his friends and planted
Their bones and grew a forest from their flesh.

And the forest is green. And the green is good.
And the good is enough. And the enough is all.

*(He gathers a pea pod. He holds it. He does not eat it. He plants it.)*

**PIP:**
For Mote. For Thorn. For all the eaten.
For all the eaters. For all the hunger
That cannot be fed. For all the yes
That became a no that became a yes.

*(He covers the pod with soil. He waters it with his tears. He waits.)*

**PIP:**
The wheel turns. But I need not turn with it.
I can be still. I can be small. I can be
Overlooked. I can be mighty in my smallness.
I can be a hampter who chose to plant
Instead of eat, to grow instead of devour,
To say yes to life instead of yes to death.

And the yes is enough. The yes is always enough.

*(He sits. He watches. He waits. The pea pod grows.)*

**CHORUS:**
The feast is ended. The famine is past.
The hampter sits alone, his lot cast
In soil and sorrow and the slow, green growth
Of pea pods planted in the earth of both
The living and the dead. The wheel has turned.
The wheel has burned. The wheel has learned
That even hunger has its end, and even
Feasts must cease, and even hampters must
Choose: to eat or to plant, to devour or to grow,
To say yes to death or yes to the slow,
Green, patient yes of the pea pod rising
From the earth, from the bones, from the ashes,
From the feast of the small.

*Exeunt omnes.*

*FINIS.*

---

*Here endeth the feast.*

*Go forth and plant pea pods.*

*And remember: thou art what thou eatest.*

*Choose wisely.*

---

**THE END**

---

*And the pea pods grew.*

*And they were bitter.*

*And they were sweet.*

*And they were enough.*
